Bees

Bees are any species classified under the clade Anthophila. There are seven families of bees: Apidae, Megachilidae, Halictidae, Andrenidae, Colletidae, Melittidae, & Stenotritidae. Bees are invertebrate animals that are insects, so they are hexapods, meaning that they have six legs.
